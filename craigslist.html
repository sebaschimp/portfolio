01 / The Familiar Problem

The need is still there. Yet hesitation has crept in.

We've all had a brush with Craigslist. Maybe your parents were hunting for a used bike, or your friend was desperate for a one of a kind apartment in San Francisco. The need is still there. People still want to look to the community for their bikes, apartments and deals. Yet hesitation has crept in.

Craigslist's once reliable reputation feels increasingly fragile with competitors like Nextdoor, OfferUp and Facebook Marketplace, each offering modern trust and safety features. Craig Newmark famously insisted that functionality mattered more than a polished interface. For years that held true, but the digital world has changed immensely since 1995.

While a facelift might seem like the obvious fix, our research, user testing and redesign revealed something deeper: the need to make trust and safety a core pillar of the Craigslist experience, and while Newmark might disagree, of course with a modern UI to match.

02 / Three Trust Barriers

Standing between users and success.

Security

Users Fear Getting Scammed

With no seller verification or accountability system, buyers had no reliable way to gauge whether a listing, or a person, was legitimate before meeting up or paying.

Usability

Users Can't Find What They Need

Inconsistent UI and redundant navigation left users unsure where to look, stifling confidence before trust was even a factor.

Credibility

Users Don't Know What's Legitimate

Minimal system feedback meant users couldn't recover from bad interactions, or tell a real listing from a risky one.

03 / Research: How We Dug Deeper

Six studies, one clear direction.

We sequenced our research so each study built on the last: evaluate the product, size up the market, ask users what matters, watch them use it, then test our redesign against the original.

01 · Expert Evaluation

Consistency was the most broken heuristic.

Using Nielsen’s usability heuristics, we found “Consistency and Standards” was the most commonly violated, and most issues clustered on the main page, listing page and item page.

01

Limited recovery

Actions like unhiding or undoing are hard to reach, with little to no system feedback.

02

Inconsistent UI

Elements vary in size and placement, and features like flagging are hidden or unclear.

03

Lack of user control

Users can’t say why they flag content, or see what happens after.

04

Unclear navigation

Redundant buttons and inconsistent categories make the site hard to navigate.

02 · Competitive Evaluation

Competitors were winning on trust.

We compared Craigslist against Nextdoor, eBay, OfferUp, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace and Amazon. Users appreciate Craigslist for no seller fees and ad-free browsing, but it lacks the trust and safety features every competitor offers.

01

Verification

Phone, email and ID verification for added trustworthiness.

02

Rating system

Buyers and sellers rate each other to build credibility.

03

Secure communication

Private conversations without sharing personal contact details.

04

Safe meetups

Designated public locations for in-person transactions.

04 · Think Aloud

One button confused everyone.

We watched 4 infrequent users search for furniture, filter results, spot potential scams and contact a seller. Messaging surfaced as a key issue for both navigation and security, which validated what we had flagged in our heuristic evaluation.

04 / The Solution

Every feature traced back to research.

We redesigned five key flows. The callouts on each screen show which study shaped that decision.

Product page

Feature 01

What we built

A verified seller badge, a Trust Score and a clear “Message Seller” button replace the confusing “Reply.”

Research behind it

Competitive analysis · Card sort · Expert evaluation

In testing

Participants found the layout easy to scan, and the number of reviews under the Trust Score made listings feel more trustworthy.

In-app messaging

Feature 02

What we built

Buyers chat in a panel beside the listing, without ever sharing a phone number or email.

Research behind it

Think aloud

In testing

5 of 5 participants preferred in-app messaging because it keeps private information private. One called it “familiar secure.”

Seller profile

Feature 03

What we built

Verified status, reputation tags and an overall rating let buyers judge a seller at a glance.

Research behind it

Competitive analysis · Closed card sort

In testing

“It has a verified and high trust score so I would feel good trusting this person.”

Writing a review

Feature 04

What we built

After a purchase, buyers rate the seller and pick up to two reputation tags.

Research behind it

Competitive analysis

In testing

5 of 5 participants felt they could successfully express their thoughts after purchasing.

Reporting a listing

Feature 05

What we built

A visible Report button, a reason for every report, and an “under review” flag on listings being inspected.

Research behind it

Expert evaluation

In testing

5 of 5 participants said the reporting layout matched their expectations.

06 / Impact & Moving Forward

From declining platform to trusted marketplace.

Trust and safety features drive higher conversion as more users complete transactions, better retention as people return to a platform they feel is secure, and a stronger reputation that attracts new users.
